Dining with intention

Small things worth knowing — grounded in what we can verify.

Locally-sourced restaurants keep more in the community
About 68¢ of every dollar spent at a restaurant that sources locally flows back into local farms, suppliers, and neighbourhood jobs — versus around 21¢ at chains, where most of the spend leaves the area. Choosing a few local meals a month meaningfully changes where your money ends up.
Find locally-sourced restaurants →
What B-Corp means — and why it matters for restaurants
A B-Corp is a business independently certified on social, environmental, and worker standards. Restaurants don't keep the badge by default — they're audited every 3 years. The “B” on the door means an active commitment to fair pay, sourcing, and lower-impact operations.
See B-Corp restaurants →
Vegetarian meals can cut your food carbon footprint by half
Plant-based dishes use far less land, water, and energy than meat. Studies show that swapping a few meals a week for vegetarian options can lower your food-related emissions by 40–50%.
Find vegetarian restaurants →

Midnight–6am charging is up to 30% cheaper
Most networks offer off-peak rates between midnight and 6am — and the grid is less stressed too. One schedule on your car or charger captures it forever.

Smaller stations often have shorter queues

Shopping with intention

Small things worth knowing — grounded in what we can verify.

B-Corps re-earn their badge every 3 years
Companies don't keep B-Corp status by default. They're audited every three years on governance, workers, community, environment, and customers. The "B" on the package means active certification.
How B-Corp audits work →
Second-hand shopping keeps clothes out of landfill
Buying pre-loved extends the life of a garment by 2× on average — saving the water, energy, and CO₂ it took to make a new one. It's also one of the easiest ways to lower your fashion footprint.
Find second-hand shops →
Why B-Corp certification matters
A B-Corp is a business independently audited on how it treats workers, customers, suppliers, the community, and the planet. The certification has to be re-earned every 3 years — so the "B" on a label means an ongoing commitment, not a one-off claim.
Find B-Corp brands →
